Meet the Matriarch

She watches the threads.

Araneae coordinates a family of defensive observation agents called Spyders. Each child has a narrow purpose, explicit scope, health state, and reporting route. They are designed to notice meaningful changes across authorized sites, repositories, Beta surfaces, recovery references, and ecosystem directories.

Araneae does not replace MAVRIC or CODalis. Her family gathers and correlates evidence; MAVRIC governs security events, while CODalis performs deeper technical diagnosis when something deserves investigation.

“Discovery is not authorization. Lineage is not authority. Suspicion is not proof.”
The Spyder Family

Small eyes. Narrow scopes. Shared awareness.

Every Spyder begins with observation authority only. New children inherit Araneae as their parent identity, never unrestricted control over the systems they observe.

Download Mac Beta First-Launch Help

First launch on macOS

  1. Download and unzip the Beta package.
  2. Read 00 START HERE - Araneae Q&A.txt.
  3. Open Launch Araneae.command.
  4. If macOS blocks it, choose Done.
  5. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  6. Scroll to Security and choose Open Anyway for Araneae.
  7. Authenticate with your password or Touch ID if requested.
  8. Return to the package and launch Araneae again.
Do not disable Gatekeeper.
“Open Anyway” approves this specific Beta while preserving the Mac's normal security protections.
Beta boundary: Araneae Desktop 0.4.0 is experimental diagnostic software, not a replacement for a certified antivirus or professional incident-response service. Diagnostic contents are not attached to Beta feedback by default.
Constitutional Rules

The family watches the web without becoming the web.

Discovery is not authorization.

Finding a site, repository, redirect, or service never automatically expands a Spyder's permitted scope.

Lineage is not authority.

Spyder children inherit identity lineage from Araneae, not unrestricted operational permissions.

Suspicion is not proof.

Unexpected change becomes something to investigate, not an automatic declaration of malware or compromise.

Private means private.

Passwords, MFA secrets, private keys, recovery secrets, and raw private CORE material never belong in this public surface.

Araneae.github.io is a public presentation and sanitized status surface. Operational crawling, authenticated repository access, private CORE consumption, incident handling, and remediation remain inside protected runtime services.
